About me

Hi, I'm Angelica.

I'm a Ukrainian-born cinematographer, director and editor: an award-winning filmmaker and photographer, music producer, songwriter, interactive web and game designer and developer, and ethical hacker.

I studied Film, Art and Anthropology at UCLA, with an exchange year of international cinema at Université Paris-Panthéon-Assas. Along the way I was lucky to learn from remarkable mentors: visionary cinema and psychomagic with Alejandro Jodorowsky, filmmaking with Christopher Coppola, cultural anthropology field work with Jared Diamond, creative writing with Ray Bradbury, and documentary and archive work with Leonard Cohen.

As a cultural anthropologist and documentary filmmaker, I spent 15 years travelling and living with Native American tribes, recording traditional knowledge, oral histories and sacred songs at the invitation of elders. I also spent more than a decade documenting the Rinzai-ji Zen Center.

Today I run Nirvana Vision, a woman-owned creative studio blending ancestral storytelling, AI-assisted filmmaking and spatial design. Off the clock you'll find me painting, DJing, making music, animating, coding, digging into OSINT puzzles or inventing things.

Filmmaking

Ancient wisdom. Neural cinema.

Award-winning cinematographer, director and editor: documentaries rooted in cultural anthropology and traditional knowledge, plus broadcast, branded content and music videos.

  • Dreams of the HydraDirector, producer, cinematographer. A surrealist short: Best Film at FLICKR LA "Attack of the 50 Foot Reels" (2007) and Echo Park Film Center "Mobile Cinema" (2008). Screened at the Egyptian Theatre, American Cinematheque and Steve Allen Theatre.
  • Cahuenga, Place of the HillDirector, producer, cinematographer. Made with the Tataviam Tribal Nation. Best Film and Best Score at Coppola's PAH Film Fest (2012).
  • The Legend of TomirisEditor and VFX artist. Best Feature Film and Best Action in a Foreign Film at the Taurus Awards, and Best Film at L'Étrange Festival (2020).
  • The Bridge ProjectA 29-tribe oral-history documentary series. I directed, filmed and edited over 100 hours of interviews with elders.
  • The World of CinemaDirector of photography, camera operator and editor for this Turkish Public Television (TRT) series.
  • Brands & music videosJade City: The Story of Jadeite (branded documentary), Saryna Key "The Crown" beauty campaign, and music videos for DJ Havana Brown.
